PHASE I – STUDENT MUST COMPLETE INDEPENDENTLY

The lecturer's Phase I instructions explicitly prohibit generative AI from researching, writing, drawing, generating, modifying or completing any part of this phase.

This folder intentionally contains no AI-generated diagram and no completed explanation.

Complete independently:
1. Choose one important MotoTrack business process.
2. Define its purpose, start, end, outcome and importance.
3. Identify actors and responsibilities.
4. Draw your own BPMN or UML Activity Diagram with swimlanes in draw.io, Lucidchart or Canva.
5. Include start/end, tasks, decisions/alternative paths, sequence/information flows and data-touching activities.
6. Identify CREATE, READ/RETRIEVE, UPDATE and DELETE/ARCHIVE operations and related data.
7. Write your own one-page explanation.
8. Keep the editable original and export the diagram and explanation as PDF.
9. Add the share link to your README and test it in a private/incognito browser.

Suggested files to create yourself:
- business_process_diagram.pdf
- business_process_explanation.pdf
- editable source file from your diagram tool

Do not submit this checklist as the Phase I deliverable.
